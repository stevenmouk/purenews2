Robot vacuums are feats of engineering capable of keeping your floors tidy with little effort, handling pet hair and dust adeptly. However, even the best robot vacuum can’t fully replace a traditional plug-in upright for deep cleaning, especially on rugs. After testing dozens of models since 2012, including 21 new models between 2023 and 2025, and spending over 80 hours examining them in multi-pet, multi-story homes, we have identified the machines that truly navigate with ease. Whether you need a smart mapping bot, a budget-friendly cleaner, or a specialist for pet hair, we’ve found the best options for you.

## 1) The best overall: Roborock Q7 M5+, ~~$359~~ $$219

<a href="https://amzn.to/4qspfLd" target="_blank"> <Image loading="lazy" src="/images/posts_img/vacuum-1.webp" alt="Roborock Q7 M5+ Robot Vacuum" height="100%" width="100%"/> </a>

<div class="outside-amazon"> <a class="find-on-amazon" href="https://amzn.to/4qspfLd" target="_blank">Find On Amazon</a>

</div>

The Roborock Q7 M5+ is our top recommendation because it balances top-notch cleaning performance with excellent navigation. It uses a combination of bump sensors and a lidar laser rangefinder to quickly map your home and identify problem areas, avoiding obstacles more effectively than most other robots we tested. In our trials, it proved to be an extremely thorough cleaner, picking up Cheerios, birdseed, and fur from both hard floors and carpets with ease.

This model comes with a user-friendly app that allows for extensive customization, such as scheduling cleaning cycles, targeting specific rooms, and creating "no-go" zones. A standout feature is the self-emptying dock, which sucks debris into a large 2.7-liter disposable bag, meaning you only need to change it every six or seven weeks. It is also one of the quietest robots we tested, making it less intrusive if you are home while it cleans.

There are minor downsides. It uses a single side brush, which makes it slightly less adept at picking up debris close to walls and thresholds compared to dual-brush models. Additionally, while Roborock’s customer service answered our calls promptly, some users have reported inconsistent support experiences online. Despite this, its reliability and smart features make it the best robot vacuum for most homes.

Specs:\
Run time: ~180 minutes (Quiet mode)\
Bin capacity: 2.7L (Dock bag)\
Smart Home: Alexa, Google Home, Siri\
Warranty: 1-year limited

## 2) Runner-up: Tapo RV30 Max Plus, $229

<a href="https://amzn.to/3MyQOnf" target="_blank"> <Image loading="lazy" src="/images/posts_img/vacuum-2.webp" alt="Tapo RV30 Max Plus Robot Vacuum" height="100%" width="100%"/> </a>

<div class="outside-amazon"> <a class="find-on-amazon" href="https://amzn.to/3MyQOnf" target="_blank">Find On Amazon</a>

</div>

If our top pick is unavailable, the Tapo RV30 Max Plus is a solid, efficient alternative that often costs less. It offers strong cleaning power and, like our top choice, includes a charging dock that automatically empties the dustbin after cleaning. Equipped with lidar navigation, it maps spaces quickly and cleans thoroughly, impressing us with its ability to pick up deep-set dust within carpets.

The RV30 Max Plus features a large 3-liter bag in its dock, which the company claims can hold three months of debris, though our testing suggests two months is more realistic for pet owners. It navigates smoothly and avoids most snags, rarely requiring human assistance to complete a run.

However, it is less refined than the Roborock. It has less suction power and its app, while functional, offers fewer customization options. It also struggled slightly more with corners and edges in our testing. Replacement parts like brushes and filters are sold as a set rather than individually, which might be inconvenient for some users.

Specs:\
Navigation: Lidar-assisted\
Bin capacity: 3L (Dock bag)\
Smart Home: Alexa, Google Home, Siri\
Warranty: 1-year limited

## 3) Best for pet hair: Eufy L60, ~~$399~~ $269

<a href="https://amzn.to/4rTXqwv" target="_blank"> <Image loading="lazy" src="/images/posts_img/vacuum-3.webp" alt="Eufy L60 Robot Vacuum" height="100%" width="100%"/> </a>

<div class="outside-amazon"> <a class="find-on-amazon" href="https://amzn.to/4rTXqwv" target="_blank">Find On Amazon</a>

</div>

For households with shedding pets, the Eufy L60 is a standout performer worth considering. In our cleaning tests, it performed on par with our top picks but truly shined when managing hair and fur. Its key advantage is the self-emptying dock, which features a smartly designed detangler built directly into the system.

This mechanism helps keep the brush roll free of wrapped hair, saving you the hassle of manually cutting strands out with scissors—a common annoyance with other robot vacuums. While it lacks a mopping function, its specialized focus on vacuuming makes it a strong contender if you can find it for around $300.

Specs:\
Special Feature: Hair detangling dock\
Navigation: Lidar\
Mopping: No\
Best for: Pet owners

## 4) Best budget option: Eufy RoboVac 11S Max, ~~$199~~ $149

<a href="https://amzn.to/3MtkJNM" target="_blank"> <Image loading="lazy" src="/images/posts_img/vacuum-4.webp" alt="Eufy RoboVac 11S Max" height="100%" width="100%"/> </a>

<div class="outside-amazon"> <a class="find-on-amazon" href="https://amzn.to/3MtkJNM" target="_blank">Find On Amazon</a>

If you want an affordable, ultra-barebones robot vacuum, the Eufy RoboVac 11S Max remains a solid choice, though it is being phased out by the manufacturer. Unlike our top picks, this model does not use smart mapping or an app; it is a "bump-and-run" style bot that navigates by gently bouncing off obstacles.

Despite its simplicity, we liked its large dustbin and strong cleaning power. It is ideal for those who don't want to fiddle with Wi-Fi setups or smartphones and just want a simple machine to help keep floors tidy. If you can find it in stock, it offers great value for a basic cleaner.

Specs:\
Control: Remote (No App)\
Navigation: Bump-and-run\
Bin Size: Large\
Status: Phasing out (limited availability)
